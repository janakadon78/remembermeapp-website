Getting started

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email and verify it. This protects your memories and enables backup across devices.

2

Unlock with Face ID

Set up Face ID or Touch ID when prompted. The app locks automatically whenever you switch away, so only you can open it.

3

Save your first memory

Type or tap the mic and say a fact — "My spare key is under the blue pot." You'll get an instant confirmation that it's stored.

4

Ask anytime

Whenever you need it, just ask — "Where's my spare key?" Remember Me answers from your saved facts in a sentence.

5

Add reminders (optional)

Link a memory to a date and choose lead times like "1 day before." Mirror it to your Calendar to get system notifications too.

Private by design

Remember Me is built so your personal facts stay personal. Here's exactly how.

🔒

Encrypted on your device

Every memory is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key held in your device's secure Keychain — never in plain text.

📱

Stored locally first

Your facts live in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They aren't sold, mined, or used for advertising.

🧠

Only what's needed for recall

When you ask a question, a bounded set of your recent facts is sent to the AI to compose an answer. Recall questions and AI replies aren't persisted beyond your session.

👤

Biometric lock

Face ID / Touch ID gates access and the app re-locks in the background, so a borrowed or lost phone doesn't expose your memories.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

What kinds of things can I store?

Anything you'd otherwise scribble on a note: where you put items, passwords and codes, parking spots, package tracking, gift ideas, a doctor's instructions. If you can say it in a sentence, Remember Me can hold onto it.

How does it know whether I'm storing or asking?

Remember Me decides your intent on-device. A statement ("My keys are on the table") is saved and confirmed instantly. A question ("Where are my keys?") is answered from your saved facts. That means the confirmation you see always matches what was actually stored.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Your memories are encrypted with AES-GCM using a key stored in the device Keychain and kept in the app's private storage. Only the facts you save are persisted; your recall questions and the assistant's replies stay in the current session. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does recall work offline?

Storing memories and on-device matching work offline. Composing a natural-language recall answer uses an AI service, which needs a connection. When you're offline you can still see your saved facts.

Can I use voice?

Yes. Tap the microphone to dictate a memory or ask a question hands-free, and the app can read answers back to you aloud.

Will my memories sync across devices?

If you sign in, your encrypted memories can be backed up and synced so they're available when you set up a new device.

What happens if I lose my phone?

The app is locked behind Face ID / Touch ID and re-locks whenever it's in the background, so your memories aren't accessible without your biometrics or device passcode.

How do I delete a memory?

Open your memories list, swipe or select the item, and delete it. Deleting removes it from your device; if sync is enabled, the change propagates to your backup.

What does it cost?

Pricing and availability are shown on the App Store listing. For questions, reach us at support@remembermeapp.online.
